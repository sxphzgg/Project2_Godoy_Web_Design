About Ethan

Ethan is my favorite person to share life and memories with. I made this page to celebrate who he is and the little things that make him special to me. <3

Ethan in a Few Little Things

Here are some details that make Ethan uniquely himself.

Something I Admire

Something I really admire about Ethan is his kindness and selflessness. He can make a friend wherever he goes. Even if he has just met someone, he talks to them as if he has known them for years.

Ethan is incredibly selfless. He always puts others before himself, especially me and his family. Over time, I have realized that Ethan lives for the people who care about him, just as he cares deeply for the people he loves.

Some of His Favorites

One of Ethan's hobbies is learning. In his free time, I often catch him watching YouTube videos about chemical reactions and other science topics. He is a chemical engineer, by the way!

We love listening to music, and he always has something playing, especially when it is quiet. Of course, he also loves to eat. Recently, I learned about his fascination with banana bread. That is what I mean when I say, “I learn more every day.”

He also goes to the gym at least three times a week, collects vinyl records, plays video games, and enjoys spending time with me, of course.

A Little Thing That Makes Me Smile

One thing that always makes me smile about Ethan is his infectious laugh. It is impossible to be around him and not smile back, even when you are having a rough day.

He tells me every day, without fail, that he loves me and that I mean everything to him. He gives me hugs and calls me “baby.” He also loves to crack a random dad joke here and there.

We make fun of each other all the time, but it is always in good fun.

One of My Favorite Memories

My favorite moment with you was the first time we went out of state together. We found out that The Strokes were playing at the Austin City Limits Festival, along with many other artists we both love.

This picture was taken in the middle of the crowd while they were performing. It was an unforgettable moment filled with emotion, especially for me. The trip was also an adventure: we walked many miles back to our hotel, relied on Ubers, and dealt with the heat throughout the day.

I love that we were able to see them again exactly six months later!
